Dior

Iconic signatures reborn in 2026 — deep history, notes & celestial alignments

Miss Dior Eau de Parfum

Expression of Femininity and Youth
Vanilla Chypre
Top: Centifolia Rose Absolute
Heart: Creamy Vanilla, Almond-toned Tonka Bean
Base: Milky Sandalwood Accord
Created in 1947 as Christian Dior’s first fragrance, dedicated to his sister Catherine. The 2026 Eau de Parfum is a modern couture reinterpretation by Francis Kurkdjian. The bottle features a hand-crafted houndstooth bow (396 threads) and optional engraving. Natalie Portman stars in the campaign celebrating love, optimism, and Grasse roses still grown from the original family estates.
Taurus • Libra

Sauvage Extrait

Authentic Masculinity
Leathery Aromatic Woody
Top: Bergamot, AOP Provence Lavender
Heart: Vietnamese Oud (leathery, animalic)
Base: Indonesian Patchouli, Madagascan Vetiver, Virginia Cedarwood
Launched 2026 as Dior’s first extrait de parfum for men, composed by Francis Kurkdjian. The formula is matured and macerated for 42 days for extreme intensity and longevity. The bottle is crystalline glass with a translucent deep-blue interior lacquer (described as a drop of ink) and engraved CD monogram on both bottle and cap. Campaign featuring Johnny Depp.
Scorpio • Aries

J’Adore Intense

Absolute Femininity
Floral Gourmand
Top: Honeyed Madagascar Ylang-Ylang
Heart: Damascena Rose, Grandiflorum Jasmine (apricot nuance)
Base: Soft Sandalwood, Generous Vanilla
2026 intensification of the 1999 J’Adore icon (originally by Calice Becker). Created by Francis Kurkdjian as a honeyed floral nectar. The amphora bottle (designed by Hervé Van der Straeten) is 53% lighter in glass weight for sustainability. Rihanna is the face of the campaign. Available in 35ml, 50ml, and 100ml.
Leo • Aquarius

Dior Homme Intense

Modern and Elegant
Ambery Woody
Top: Tuscan Iris Butter
Heart: Ecuadorian Ambrette Seed Absolute
Base: Virginia Cedarwood
The olfactory expression of a sharply tailored modern Dior suit. Powdery, elegant iris is elevated with a sensual ambery facet and precious woods. Campaign starring Robert Pattinson under the title “I’m Your Man.” Refined, confident, and sophisticated with excellent longevity.

Good Girl & Paco Rabanne

Iconic bottles that redefined modern fragrance

Good Girl

Bold, Feminine, Seductive
Ambery Floral
Top: Bitter Almond
Heart: Jasmine Sambac, Creamy Tuberose
Base: Roasted Tonka Bean, Cocoa
Launched in 2016 by Carolina Herrera and composed by Louise Turner. The midnight-blue stiletto bottle (gold heel) broke every traditional perfume design rule and became a global symbol of feminine power, duality, and confidence. The fragrance celebrates the modern woman who is both sweet and powerful. Widely available at Ulta.
Scorpio • Leo

Invictus / 1 Million

Victorious, Confident, Iconic
Fresh Marine / Sweet Amber
Invictus: Grapefruit, Marine Accord, Guaiac Wood, Patchouli, Ambergris
1 Million: Iconic gold-bar bottle of luxury and excess
Invictus (2013) is the silver trophy bottle inspired by sporting victory and the energy of winning. 1 Million (2008) made the gold ingot a worldwide symbol of masculine luxury and success. Both lines are core to Rabanne’s identity and widely stocked at Ulta.
