Dior

The house of Christian Dior — from the New Look at 30 Avenue Montaigne to the fragrances that followed.

Christian Dior

Founder of the House of Dior. His first fragrance, Miss Dior (1947), was dedicated to his sister Catherine. The house remains at its original address on Avenue Montaigne in Paris.

30 Avenue Montaigne

The original Dior couture house in Paris. Christian Dior moved in in 1946. This is where the New Look was born in 1947 and where the first Miss Dior fragrance was developed. The historic hôtel particulier still stands as the heart of the house.

Miss Dior Eau de Parfum

Vanilla Chypre
Centifolia Rose Absolute • Creamy Vanilla & Almond Tonka • Milky Sandalwood
Face: Natalie Portman (since 2011) • Perfumer: Francis Kurkdjian
Origins & Development: Created in 1947 in Paris as Christian Dior’s first fragrance, dedicated to his sister Catherine Dior. The 2026 version by Francis Kurkdjian elevates the original rose chypre. The bottle features a houndstooth bow inspired by the New Look.

Sauvage Extrait

Leathery Aromatic Woody
Vietnamese Oud • Patchouli • Vetiver • Cedar • Lavender
Face: Johnny Depp (since 2015) • Perfumer: Francis Kurkdjian
Origins & Development: Dior’s first extrait for men, 2026. Macerated 42 days. Deep-blue bottle with the CD monogram.

Estée Lauder

From a Queens kitchen to a global beauty empire.

Estée Lauder

Born Josephine Esther Mentzer, 1908, Corona, Queens, New York
Co-founded the company with her husband Joseph in 1946. Started with creams made by her uncle in the family kitchen. Opened the first counter at Saks Fifth Avenue. The only woman on Time’s 1998 list of the 20 most influential business geniuses of the 20th century. Presidential Medal of Freedom, 2004.

Beautiful Belle

Classic Floral
Lychee • Rose • Mimosa • Orange Flower • Gardenia • Tuberose
Origins: Beautiful (1985) was the last fragrance Estée Lauder personally worked on with her daughter-in-law Evelyn. Romantic bridal alternative to the big sexy scents of the 1980s.

Lancôme

A French rose and a Parisian dream.

La Vie Est Belle

Floral Gourmand
Iris • Patchouli • Jasmine • Praline • Vanilla
Face: Julia Roberts • Perfumers: Anne Flipo, Dominique Ropion, Olivier Polge
Origins: 2012. Crystal smile bottle revived from a 1949 archive design by Georges Delhomme. Julia Roberts has been the face since launch. Still one of the top-selling women’s fragrances in France.

Armand Petitjean

Founder, 1935 • Paris
Founded Lancôme in 1935 after working with François Coty. Named the house after the Château de Lancosme in the Brenne region of France; the golden rose symbol comes from the roses grown there. Launched the first five fragrances at the 1935 Brussels World’s Fair. Acquired by L’Oréal in 1964.

Clinique

Happy

Fresh Floral
Grapefruit • Mandarin • Hawaiian Wedding Flower • Mimosa
Origins: 1997. Created as a deliberate contrast to the big 80s power florals. Still a bestseller.

Tate McRae

TATE 01

Fresh Aromatic Woody
Sparkling Bergamot • Clary Sage • Orange Flower • Hedione • Iso E Super • Sensual Moss • Creamy Tonka
Origins: 2026 debut fragrance by Tate McRae with Scent Beauty. Minimal milky-cube bottle. Available at Ulta. Developed as a “second skin” scent.
