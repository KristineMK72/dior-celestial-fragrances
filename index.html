Dior

Iconic signatures reborn in 2026 — deep history, people & origins

Miss Dior Eau de Parfum

Expression of Femininity and Youth
Vanilla Chypre
Centifolia Rose Absolute • Creamy Vanilla & Almond Tonka • Milky Sandalwood
Face: Natalie Portman (since 2011) • Perfumer: Francis Kurkdjian • Founder: Christian Dior
Origins & Development: Created in 1947 in Paris as Christian Dior’s first fragrance, dedicated to his beloved sister Catherine Dior, a French Resistance fighter. Developed at the house’s laboratories under the original artistic direction of the couture house at 30 Avenue Montaigne. The 2026 Eau de Parfum is a modern couture reinterpretation by Francis Kurkdjian, elevating the original rose chypre with creamy vanilla and milky sandalwood. The bottle features a hand-crafted houndstooth bow inspired by Dior’s New Look.

The People: Natalie Portman has been the global face since 2011. The 2026 campaign shows her gathering flowers in Paris.

Vietnamese Oud • Indonesian Patchouli • Madagascan Vetiver • Virginia Cedar • Provence Lavender
Face: Johnny Depp (since 2015) • Perfumer: Francis Kurkdjian
Origins & Development: Dior’s first extrait de parfum for men, launched in 2026. Developed in Paris under Francis Kurkdjian’s direction and macerated for 42 days. The bottle features deep-blue interior lacquer and the CD monogram, nodding to the original Sauvage (2015).

Tate McRae

Her debut fragrance — a second skin

TATE 01 Eau de Parfum

Subtle Second-Skin Aura
Fresh Aromatic Woody
Sparkling Bergamot • Clary Sage • Orange Flower • Hedione • Iso E Super • Sensual Moss • Creamy Tonka
Creator & Face: Tate McRae • Perfumers: Adriana Medina & Antoine Maisondieu (with Scent Beauty / Givaudan)
Origins & Development: Launched October 2026 as Tate McRae’s first fragrance, in partnership with Scent Beauty. Designed as a “second skin” that blends with the wearer’s own chemistry (thanks to Iso E Super) rather than projecting loudly. Tate was deeply involved in formulation meetings and chose the minimal milky-cube bottle herself, comparing the process to songwriting — she went through countless options until it felt genuine and timeless. Exclusive to Ulta Beauty (online mid-October, in stores late October).

The People: Entirely driven by Tate McRae’s vision of subtlety and individuality. The clean, modern bottle reflects her aesthetic: simple at first glance, complex up close.
