Dior

Iconic signatures reborn in 2026 — deep history, people & origins

Miss Dior Eau de Parfum

Expression of Femininity and Youth
Vanilla Chypre
Centifolia Rose Absolute • Creamy Vanilla & Almond Tonka • Milky Sandalwood
Face: Natalie Portman (since 2011) • Perfumer: Francis Kurkdjian • Founder: Christian Dior
Origins & Development: Created in 1947 in Paris as Christian Dior’s first fragrance, dedicated to his beloved sister Catherine Dior, a French Resistance fighter. Developed at the house’s laboratories under the original artistic direction of the couture house at 30 Avenue Montaigne. The 2026 Eau de Parfum is a modern couture reinterpretation by Francis Kurkdjian, elevating the original rose chypre with creamy vanilla and milky sandalwood. The bottle features a hand-crafted houndstooth bow inspired by Dior’s New Look.

The People: Natalie Portman has been the global face since 2011. The 2026 campaign (directed by Manu Cossu, photographed by Lachlan Bailey) shows her gathering flowers in Paris to Chappell Roan’s “Good Luck Babe.”
Taurus • Libra

Sauvage Extrait

Authentic Masculinity
Leathery Aromatic Woody
Vietnamese Oud • Indonesian Patchouli • Madagascan Vetiver • Virginia Cedar • Provence Lavender
Face: Johnny Depp (since 2015) • Perfumer: Francis Kurkdjian • Photographer: Jean-Baptiste Mondino
Origins & Development: Dior’s first extrait de parfum for men, launched in 2026. Developed in Paris under Francis Kurkdjian’s direction and macerated for 42 days for exceptional concentration. The bottle features deep-blue interior lacquer and the CD monogram, nodding to the original Sauvage (2015) which was created to capture the spirit of open landscapes.

The People: Johnny Depp has been the face since 2015. The 2026 campaign, photographed by Jean-Baptiste Mondino, shows him in a contemplative, intense mode that mirrors the fragrance’s power.
Scorpio • Aries

J’Adore Intense

Absolute Femininity
Floral Gourmand
Honeyed Ylang-Ylang • Damascena Rose • Jasmine with Apricot • Sandalwood & Vanilla
Face / Muse: Rihanna (since 2024) • Perfumer: Francis Kurkdjian • Original: Calice Becker (1999)
Origins & Development: The original J’Adore was created in 1999 by Calice Becker and launched as Dior’s modern floral icon. The 2026 Intense is a new composition by Francis Kurkdjian, developed specifically with Rihanna as muse — the first time he has composed with a face in mind. The amphora bottle is 53% lighter. Developed in Paris, the scent intensifies the original with honeyed ylang and apricot-touched jasmine.

The People: Rihanna succeeded Charlize Theron as the face in 2024. The campaign was shot at the Palace of Versailles by Steven Klein.
Leo • Aquarius

Dior Homme Intense

Modern and Elegant
Ambery Woody
Tuscan Iris Butter • Ecuadorian Ambrette • Virginia Cedar
Face: Robert Pattinson (multiple campaigns) • Photographer: Peter Lindbergh (earlier), recent films by The Blaze
Origins & Development: The olfactory interpretation of a sharp modern Dior suit. Originally developed in Paris as part of the Dior Homme line (launched 2005 by Hedi Slimane). The Intense version elevates the powdery iris with sensual amber and precious woods. Campaigns have been shot in New York and Paris.

The People: Robert Pattinson has fronted Dior Homme campaigns since the mid-2010s, including the 2016 Intense City campaign photographed by Peter Lindbergh in New York. Recent films directed by The Blaze continue the urban, magnetic aesthetic.
Virgo • Capricorn

Good Girl & Paco Rabanne

Iconic bottles that redefined modern fragrance

Good Girl

Bold, Feminine, Seductive
Ambery Floral
Bitter Almond • Jasmine Sambac • Tuberose • Roasted Tonka • Cocoa
Founder: Carolina Herrera • Perfumer: Louise Turner • Face: Karlie Kloss (since 2016, 10-year anniversary in 2026)
Origins & Development: Launched in 2016 by the House of Carolina Herrera in New York. Fragrance Creative Director Carolina A. Herrera worked with perfumers Louise Turner and Quentin Bisch to create a scent built on contrast: bright jasmine and tuberose against roasted tonka and cocoa. The midnight-blue stiletto bottle (gold heel) was a radical design break and instantly became a global symbol of feminine power and duality. Developed as a celebration of the multifaceted modern woman.

The People: Karlie Kloss has been the face since day one. In 2026 she celebrated a decade with the brand, including a V Magazine cover story. The house has also supported her Kode With Klossy coding nonprofit.
Scorpio • Leo

Good Girl Blush

Radiant, Powdery, Feminine
Fruity Floral Amber
Bergamot & Bitter Almond • Ylang-Ylang & Peony • Vanilla & Tonka
Perfumers: Quentin Bisch, Shyamala Maisondieu, Christophe Raynaud • Face: Karlie Kloss
Origins & Development: Launched in 2023 as a brighter, more floral chapter in the Good Girl line. Quentin Bisch was inspired by the pink stiletto bottle — he saw the gold heel as a vanilla pod and built the fragrance around a double dose of Madagascar vanilla (gourmande and powdery extractions). Ylang-ylang and peony create a luminous heart. Key ingredients were ethically sourced from Madagascar and the Comoros Islands through a Givaudan Sourcing4Good partnership, with Carolina A. Herrera visiting the origin communities.

The People: Karlie Kloss continued as face, appearing in soft pink and 1980s-inspired looks. The scent celebrates the power of pink and the duality of modern femininity.
Libra • Pisces

Invictus / 1 Million

Victorious, Confident, Iconic
Fresh Marine / Sweet Amber
Invictus: Grapefruit, Marine, Guaiac Wood • 1 Million: Blood Mandarin, Cinnamon, Leather
Founder: Paco Rabanne • 1 Million bottle by Noé Duchaufour-Lawrance • Invictus bottle by Cédric Ragot
Origins & Development: 1 Million (2008) was created in Paris by the House of Paco Rabanne by perfumers Christophe Raynaud, Olivier Pescheux, and Michel Girard. The gold-ingot bottle (designed by Noé Duchaufour-Lawrance) became a worldwide symbol of masculine luxury, nodding to Paco Rabanne’s metallic couture of the 1960s. Invictus (2013) followed with a silver trophy bottle designed by Cédric Ragot, inspired by sporting victory. Both remain core Ulta bestsellers and have spawned large flanker families.
Aries • Leo

Ulta Favorites

Beloved bestsellers you can find at Ulta Beauty — verified official bottles

Viktor & Rolf Flowerbomb

Explosive Floral, Confident, Addictive
Floral Oriental
Tea, Bergamot • Jasmine, Rose, Orchid • Patchouli, Vanilla
Designers: Viktor Horsting & Rolf Snoeren • Perfumer: Olivier Polge / Carlos Benaim / Dominique Ropion • Bottle: Fabien Baron
Origins & Development: Launched in 2005 by the Dutch design duo Viktor Horsting and Rolf Snoeren (founded 1993 in Paris after meeting at Arnhem Academy). The scent was developed as an “explosion of 1,000 flowers.” The iconic diamond-grenade bottle was designed by Fabien Baron. Created in collaboration with master perfumers Olivier Polge, Carlos Benaim, and Dominique Ropion. One of Ulta’s all-time best-selling florals and the start of the “bomb” concept for the house.
Leo • Scorpio

Viktor & Rolf Spicebomb

Spicy, Bold, Masculine
Woody Spicy
Pink Pepper, Bergamot • Cinnamon, Saffron, Chili • Tobacco, Leather, Vetiver
Designers: Viktor Horsting & Rolf Snoeren • Perfumer: Olivier Polge • Bottle: Fabien Baron • Campaign: Sean O’Pry
Origins & Development: Launched in 2012 as the masculine counterpart to Flowerbomb. Viktor and Rolf said they were “jealous of all the women who had Flowerbomb” and wanted men to have their own bomb. Olivier Polge (then at IFF) created two opposing accords: an icy explosive top and a fiery addictive base. The grenade bottle (also by Fabien Baron) features the V&R seal as the pin. Campaign face was model Sean O’Pry. A major Ulta masculine bestseller with multiple flankers.
Aries • Scorpio

YSL Black Opium

Addictive, Sensual, Nighttime
Oriental Vanilla
Pink Pepper, Orange Blossom • Coffee, Jasmine • Vanilla, Patchouli
House: Yves Saint Laurent • Perfumer: Olivier Cresp / Honorine Blanc / Nathalie Lorson
Origins & Development: Launched in 2014 by Yves Saint Laurent Beauty as a modern, edgy reinterpretation of the original Opium (1977). Developed as the first major feminine fragrance to feature a black coffee accord. The black glitter bottle with pink center was designed to evoke nightlife and sequins. Created by a team of perfumers including Olivier Cresp, Honorine Blanc, and Nathalie Lorson. A top Ulta seller for years.
Scorpio • Capricorn

YSL Libre

Free, Bold, Floral Lavender
Floral Lavender
Lavender, Mandarin • Orange Blossom, Jasmine • Vanilla, Cedar, Musk
Perfumers: Anne Flipo & Carlos Benaïm • Face: Dua Lipa
Origins & Development: Launched in August 2019 after eight years of development (beginning 2011) and more than 1,500 formula versions. Anne Flipo (Paris) and Carlos Benaïm (New York) worked separately then refined together. They took the traditionally masculine fougère structure (lavender) and made it feminine with Moroccan orange blossom and Madagascar vanilla. The name “Libre” (Free) honors Yves Saint Laurent’s legacy of dressing women in trouser suits. Dua Lipa has been the face. A modern Ulta pillar.
Aquarius • Libra

Ariana Grande Cloud

Sweet, Dreamy, Youthful
Gourmand Floral
Lavender, Pear • Coconut, Praline • Musk, Woods
Face / Founder: Ariana Grande
Origins & Development: Launched in 2018 as Ariana Grande’s first major fragrance. Developed as an uplifting, artistic expression of positivity. The cloud-shaped bottle was designed to evoke both natural clouds and digital optimism. Created in partnership with a major fragrance house and quickly became a Gen-Z phenomenon at Ulta and beyond. Soft, sweet, and airy with strong skin chemistry performance.
Pisces • Cancer

Marc Jacobs Daisy

Fresh, Playful, Optimistic
Floral Fruity
Wild Strawberry, Violet • Jasmine, Gardenia • Musk, Vanilla
Designer: Marc Jacobs • Perfumer: Alberto Morillas
Origins & Development: Launched in 2007 by Marc Jacobs Fragrances. Created by master perfumer Alberto Morillas as a radiant floral celebrating youthful optimism. The white daisy-cap bottle became an instant cultural icon of playful femininity and won Fragrance Foundation awards for packaging. Developed as a bright, approachable scent that has spawned numerous flankers and remains a long-running Ulta bestseller.

Tate McRae — TATE 01

The pop star’s debut fragrance and her rising Victoria’s Secret moment

TATE 01

Effortless, Subtly Edgy, Second Skin
Woody Floral
Sparkling Bergamot • Clary Sage • White Florals (Orange Flower, Hedione) • Moss • Creamy Tonka • Sandalwood • Iso E Super
Founder / Face: Tate McRae • Perfumers: Adriana Medina & Antoine Maisondieu (Givaudan / Scent Beauty) • Campaign photographer: Mario Sorrenti
Origins & Development: Launched October 2026 in partnership with Scent Beauty. Developed by Givaudan master perfumers Adriana Medina and Antoine Maisondieu as a “second skin” fragrance that interacts with the wearer’s chemistry via Iso E Super. The sculptural glass bottle balances soft fluidity with architectural strength. Campaign shot by Mario Sorrenti in a 90s supermodel aesthetic. Available on tatebytm.com ($30–$82) and Ulta (online Oct 16, stores Oct 25).

Victoria’s Secret connection: On October 18, 2026, Tate performed at the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show in Los Angeles in a custom red lingerie look (corset, panties, garters), debuting “Housewife” live and also performing “Sports Car” and “Dear God.” She walked the runway, shared a moment with Gigi Hadid, and wore wings for the closer. She has also worn VS pieces in personal style moments, including a viral $50 lace balconette bra.
Gemini • Libra
