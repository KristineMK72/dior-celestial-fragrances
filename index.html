Dior

Iconic signatures reborn in 2026 — verified images, deep history, people & notes

Miss Dior Eau de Parfum

Expression of Femininity and Youth
Vanilla Chypre
Centifolia Rose Absolute • Creamy Vanilla & Almond Tonka • Milky Sandalwood
Face: Natalie Portman (since 2011) • Perfumer: Francis Kurkdjian • Founder: Christian Dior
Created in 1947 as Christian Dior’s first fragrance, dedicated to his sister Catherine. The 2026 Eau de Parfum is a modern couture reinterpretation by Francis Kurkdjian. The bottle features a hand-crafted houndstooth bow. Natalie Portman has embodied Miss Dior for over 15 years; the 2026 campaign (directed by Manu Cossu, photographed by Lachlan Bailey) shows her in Paris gathering flowers to Chappell Roan’s “Good Luck Babe.”
Taurus • Libra

Sauvage Extrait

Authentic Masculinity
Leathery Aromatic Woody
Vietnamese Oud • Indonesian Patchouli • Madagascan Vetiver • Virginia Cedar • Provence Lavender
Face: Johnny Depp (since 2015) • Perfumer: Francis Kurkdjian • Photographer: Jean-Baptiste Mondino
Dior’s first extrait de parfum for men (2026). Macerated 42 days. Bottle with deep-blue interior lacquer and CD monogram. Johnny Depp returns as ambassador in a contemplative campaign that mirrors the fragrance’s intensity.
Scorpio • Aries

J’Adore Intense

Absolute Femininity
Floral Gourmand
Honeyed Ylang-Ylang • Damascena Rose • Jasmine with Apricot • Sandalwood & Vanilla
Face / Muse: Rihanna (since 2024) • Perfumer: Francis Kurkdjian • Original: Calice Becker (1999)
2026 intensification of the 1999 icon. Francis Kurkdjian composed it with Rihanna specifically in mind — the first time he has used a muse. The amphora is 53% lighter. Rihanna succeeded Charlize Theron; campaign shot at Versailles by Steven Klein. Honeyed, voluptuous, and solar.
Leo • Aquarius

Dior Homme Intense

Modern and Elegant
Ambery Woody
Tuscan Iris Butter • Ecuadorian Ambrette • Virginia Cedar
Face: Robert Pattinson (multiple campaigns) • Photographer: Peter Lindbergh (earlier), recent films by The Blaze
The olfactory interpretation of a sharp modern Dior suit. Powdery iris elevated with sensual amber and precious woods. Robert Pattinson has fronted Dior Homme campaigns since the mid-2010s, including Intense City. Refined, confident, sophisticated.

Good Girl & Paco Rabanne

Iconic bottles that redefined modern fragrance

Good Girl

Bold, Feminine, Seductive
Ambery Floral
Bitter Almond • Jasmine Sambac • Tuberose • Roasted Tonka • Cocoa
Founder: Carolina Herrera • Perfumer: Louise Turner • Face: Karlie Kloss (since 2016, 10-year anniversary in 2026)
Launched 2016. The midnight-blue stiletto bottle (gold heel) broke every traditional design rule and became a global symbol of feminine power and duality. Karlie Kloss has been the face for a decade and recently celebrated the milestone with V Magazine. Widely available at Ulta.

Invictus / 1 Million

Victorious, Confident, Iconic
Fresh Marine / Sweet Amber
Invictus: Grapefruit, Marine, Guaiac Wood • 1 Million: Gold-bar luxury
Founder: Paco Rabanne • Invictus bottle by Cédric Ragot

Tate McRae — TATE 01

The pop star’s debut fragrance and her rising Victoria’s Secret moment

TATE 01

Effortless, Subtly Edgy, Second Skin
Woody Floral
Sparkling Bergamot • Clary Sage • White Florals (Orange Flower, Hedione) • Moss • Creamy Tonka • Sandalwood • Iso E Super
Founder / Face: Tate McRae • Perfumers: Adriana Medina & Antoine Maisondieu (Givaudan / Scent Beauty) • Campaign photographer: Mario Sorrenti
Launched October 2026 with Scent Beauty. Designed as a “second skin” that interacts with the wearer’s chemistry via Iso E Super. Available on tatebytm.com ($30–$82) and Ulta (online Oct 16, stores Oct 25). Campaign shot by Mario Sorrenti in a 90s supermodel aesthetic.

Victoria’s Secret connection: On October 18, 2026, Tate performed at the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show in Los Angeles in a custom red lingerie look (corset, panties, garters), debuting “Housewife” live and also performing “Sports Car” and “Dear God.” She walked the runway, shared a moment with Gigi Hadid, and wore wings for the closer. She has also worn VS pieces in personal style moments, including a viral $50 lace balconette bra.
Gemini • Libra
