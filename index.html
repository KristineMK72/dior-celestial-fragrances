Dior

Iconic signatures reborn in 2026 — deep history, people & origins

Miss Dior Eau de Parfum

Expression of Femininity and Youth
Vanilla Chypre
Centifolia Rose Absolute • Creamy Vanilla & Almond Tonka • Milky Sandalwood
Face: Natalie Portman (since 2011) • Perfumer: Francis Kurkdjian • Founder: Christian Dior
Origins & Development: Created in 1947 in Paris as Christian Dior’s first fragrance, dedicated to his beloved sister Catherine Dior, a French Resistance fighter. Developed at the house’s laboratories under the original artistic direction of the couture house at 30 Avenue Montaigne. The 2026 Eau de Parfum is a modern couture reinterpretation by Francis Kurkdjian.

Armani

Mediterranean light, marine freshness, and the elegance of Giorgio Armani

Acqua di Giò

Fresh Mediterranean Masculinity
Aromatic Aquatic
Bergamot • Marine Notes • Sage • Lavender • Vetiver • Cedar
Perfumer: Alberto Morillas (with Annick Ménardo & Christian Dussoulier) • Inspired by Giorgio Armani’s holidays on Pantelleria • Faces: Larry Scott, Lars Burmeister, Aaron Taylor-Johnson
Origins & Development: Launched in 1996 (men’s version; women’s in 1995). Inspired by the volcanic island of Pantelleria between Sicily and Tunisia, where Giorgio Armani spent vacations. Alberto Morillas captured the scent of the Mediterranean sea, bergamot, and mineral freshness. The simple frosted bottle with silver cap was designed by Fabien Baron. It became one of the best-selling men’s fragrances worldwide, with a bottle sold roughly every few seconds at peak.

The People: Iconic black-and-white campaigns by Herb Ritts, Peter Lindbergh, and others featuring models diving into the sea. Morillas has said opening the bottle makes him see the blue of the Mediterranean.

Woody Leathery
Violet • Leather • Marine Accord • Green Aromatics
Continuation of the Acqua di Giò line under Armani Beauty
Origins & Development: The most concentrated expression in the Acqua di Giò family, pushing marine freshness into deeper, more intense territory with violet and leather accords. The sleek black-and-silver bottle emphasizes mystery and power while staying true to the original sea inspiration from Pantelleria.

Dolce & Gabbana

Sicilian summers and Capri light in a bottle

Light Blue

Bright Mediterranean Joy
Citrus Floral
Sicilian Lemon • Green Apple • Bellflower • Jasmine • Cedar • Musk
Founders: Domenico Dolce & Stefano Gabbana • Perfumers: Olivier Cresp & Alberto Morillas • Faces: Bianca Balti & David Gandy; recent Vittoria Ceretti & Theo James
Origins & Development: Launched in 2001 for women (men’s 2007). Inspired by Domenico Dolce’s Sicilian roots and the island of Capri. Olivier Cresp and Alberto Morillas spent over two years perfecting the bright lemon-apple opening that became synonymous with summer. The angular frosted bottle evokes Mediterranean light. The 2025 25th-anniversary relaunch intensified longevity while keeping the DNA, with new majolica-inspired packaging.

The People: Iconic campaigns shot in Capri featuring Bianca Balti and David Gandy in retro white swimwear. The scent is a hymn to the Mediterranean coast.

Billie Eilish

Intimate, body-inspired scents from the artist herself

Eilish Eau de Parfum

Warm Intimate Gourmand
Amber Gourmand
Sugared Petals • Mandarin • Creamy Vanilla • Cocoa • Warm Musks • Tonka
Creator & Face: Billie Eilish • Perfumer: Steve DeMercado
Origins & Development: Launched in 2021. Billie Eilish wanted a scent that didn’t exist — vanilla, amber, musk, cocoa, rose, and wood that felt intimate and human. She worked closely with the formula; when she smelled the final sample she nearly cried. The metallic gold bottle is a sculptural bust of a figure from the chest up, head turned, celebrating the body and intimacy. Developed as a personal necessity that became a global bestseller at Ulta.

The People: Entirely driven by Billie Eilish’s vision of closeness and self. Subsequent releases (No. 2, No. 3) expanded the line while keeping the architectural bottle language.

Yves Saint Laurent

Freedom, gender-bending elegance, and the spirit of Yves

Libre Eau de Parfum

Free, Floral, Empowered
Floral Fougère
Lavender • Orange Blossom • Jasmine • Madagascar Vanilla • Cedar • Musk
Perfumers: Anne Flipo & Carlos Benaïm • Bottle: Suzanne Dalton • Face: Dua Lipa
Origins & Development: Development began around 2011; over 1,500–1,750 versions were created before the 2019 launch. Anne Flipo and Carlos Benaïm worked across the Atlantic to turn a classic masculine fougère structure into a feminine floral addiction by blending French lavender with Moroccan orange blossom. The bottle by Suzanne Dalton places the iconic Cassandre YSL monogram on its side like a jewel, with a gold chain and asymmetric black cap — sober glass meeting wild accessory, echoing Yves’ own philosophy.

The People: Dua Lipa became the face, photographed by Nabil Elderkin. The scent is a freestyle take on freedom and the tuxedo-for-women spirit of Yves Saint Laurent.
