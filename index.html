Dior

The house of Christian Dior — from the New Look at 30 Avenue Montaigne to the fragrances that followed.

Christian Dior

Founder of the House of Dior. His first fragrance, Miss Dior (1947), was dedicated to his sister Catherine. The house remains at its original address on Avenue Montaigne in Paris.

30 Avenue Montaigne

The original Dior couture house in Paris. This is where the New Look was born in 1947 and where the first Miss Dior fragrance was developed.

Miss Dior Eau de Parfum

Vanilla Chypre
Centifolia Rose Absolute • Creamy Vanilla & Almond Tonka • Milky Sandalwood
Face: Natalie Portman (since 2011) • Perfumer: Francis Kurkdjian
Origins & Development: Created in 1947 in Paris as Christian Dior’s first fragrance, dedicated to his sister Catherine Dior. The 2026 version by Francis Kurkdjian elevates the original rose chypre. The bottle features a houndstooth bow inspired by the New Look.

Sauvage Extrait

Leathery Aromatic Woody
Vietnamese Oud • Patchouli • Vetiver • Cedar • Lavender
Face: Johnny Depp (since 2015) • Perfumer: Francis Kurkdjian
Origins & Development: Dior’s first extrait for men, 2026. Macerated 42 days. Deep-blue bottle with the CD monogram.

Estée Lauder
